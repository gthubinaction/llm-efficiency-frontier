Provenance. Production-function parameters replicate the published estimation protocol of Besiroglu et al. (2024) on their reconstruction of Hoffmann et al. (2022) — α=0.3473, β=0.3672, E=1.8172 — and are frozen; every quantity here derives from them in closed form. Envelope arms are fitted to the 15-point empirical frontier. Observed range N ∈ [5.7×10⁷, 1.6×10¹⁰].

Scope. Training compute only; inference, engineering and data acquisition are excluded. The hurdle and the value of performance are organizational inputs, not universal constants. Transparent decision support, not an oracle.
